# Position: where the family stands in the field

Step 4. "Nobody has done this" is kept apart from "we have not done this here".

**Shape:** [position](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/position.md)

## Which field

- **Intelligence augmentation** is the root: a person decides, tools extend what the person can do (Engelbart 1962; Licklider 1960).
- **Hybrid intelligence** is the closest living community, with an agenda of four challenges (Akata 2020).
- **Human-AI teaming** fits only in part: its model is a teammate; here the model decides nothing.
- **Human-in-the-loop** is a neighbour, about training; it does not fit a project.

## Name

The field says augmentation. The prefix "AI-augmented" matches it.
Recommendation: keep the prefix and the hub's name; a rename to a field term is the maintainer's choice and not needed.

## What is known

- The split, person sets goals and machine does routine work, is sixty years old.
- Levels of automation by function are standard (Parasuraman 2000).
- Combinations beat either alone on content creation and lose on decisions (Vaccaro 2024).

## What is not known

- No source found treats the checking machine (tests, CI, a proof assistant) as a third role beside the person and the model. The literature has two, human and AI. In Parasuraman's terms it is automation of analysis. This is from the pool read, not a claim about the world.
- No source found measures a rule that the model decides nothing.

## What this family adds

- A third role, the machine that checks, with an exact verdict.
- One repository per problem, with a program that checks that scopes do not overlap.
- Cases measured as human alone, model alone and combination, which [against](against.md) asks of any claim.
