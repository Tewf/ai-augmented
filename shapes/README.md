# Shapes

A **shape** is the fixed skeleton of one kind of document: every document of that kind has the same parts in the same
order. This is the one particular to the family. The general ones (note, folder README, report, commit message,
decision record) are in
[reader-ergonomics](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/b9732b9814c71768f722691f3c5049ec2b8ed378/shapes/README.md).

## Contents

| Shape | For |
|---|---|
| [scope-table.md](scope-table.md) | what one member holds: a table of in, later and out rows, each with its reason, in the form the overlap check reads |

## Choosing a shape

- One document, one shape: a text that needs two shapes is two documents.
- A document names its shape on a line `**Shape:**` that links the shape's file; the checks find it that way.
